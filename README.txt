This is a small game coded in C++ with Raylib.

The aim of the game is to get an ak-47. You also begin as a single-celled organism and can evolve into different species to gain health, speed, and other things to give you an edge.

You upgrade your weapon with Gold and you "evolve" so to speak using Xp. Both of these are obtained through killing other organisms.

Weapons have different loading mechanisms, different loading speeds, and deal different amounts of damage.

I hope you like the game!
